ISTE+ASCD Brand System Discovery Survey

We're building a brand system and governance framework for ISTE+ASCD: a clear hierarchy for our logos, sub-brands, and program marks, plus guidance on when to create new ones and who approves them.

The goal is to make your work easier, so you're not inventing a brand from scratch every time you launch something.

Before we design anything, we want to hear from the people closest to the work. The survey takes about 10 minutes. Most questions are open-ended, and there are no wrong answers.
